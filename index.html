Stop reconciling consignment stock by hand

A focused Shopify app for shops that mix owned inventory with consigned stock — from other makers, or out at partner retailers.

If you restock consigned and owned inventory together, Shopify's stock counts and your consignor reports drift apart fast — and getting them back in sync (without a duplicate vendor payment) is a manual, error-prone chore every cycle.

We're building a narrow tool that does one thing: reconcile consignment-reported sales and restocks back into your Shopify inventory automatically. No payout automation, no consignor portal to manage — just accurate stock counts.

If that's a problem you have, we'd like to hear from you and, if it fits, put together a small paid pilot.

We'll only use this to follow up about the pilot — no newsletter, nothing shared or sold. One or two emails, then you're either in the pilot or we tell you we're not building it.
